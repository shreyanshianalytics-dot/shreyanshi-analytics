About

Analytics that make business sense

Shreyanshi Analytics is a data analytics practice built to help teams see what is happening, why it matters, and what to do next. The work sits between raw data and the decisions leaders actually need to make.

We design dashboards, reports and analysis around your questions—not around a generic template. Whether the source is a spreadsheet, a SQL database or a mix of both, the goal is the same: clarity you can act on.

Analytics process

From question to insight

A clear path from the business problem to a dashboard, report or recommendation you can use.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Define the decision, the audience and the question the data must answer.

  2. 02

    Collect

    Identify the right sources in Excel, SQL, Power BI or existing files.

  3. 03

    Analyze

    Clean, model and explore the data until the story is reliable.

  4. 04

    Visualize

    Build dashboards and reports that leaders can read at a glance.

  5. 05

    Recommend

    Translate findings into next steps, not just another chart pack.
